Privacy — restline.app

What this page collects. A random id in a cookie (rl_v), kept 90 days, so one visitor isn't counted twice. Seven events — page viewed, clip played or not, price card scrolled into view, price button tapped (and which of the two), email submitted, answer submitted, page left (seconds open) — each with the time, the campaign tags in the link (utm), the device type, the browser's identification string, whether the page opened inside the TikTok, Instagram or Facebook app, and the domain the link was opened from. An email address, only if one is typed in. A one-line answer, only if one is typed in.

Why. To count how many visitors from the ads tap the price, to check the page works inside the apps, and to send one email on the day the app is released.

What isn't collected. No name, no location, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no pixels. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

How long. Counts and emails are deleted 30 days after the test ends. An email address is deleted on request at any time.

Where. The page and its counter run on Netlify; the data is stored there, in the United States.

Contact. hello@restline.app
